Stop Wasting Time Browsing

Ever spend 30 minutes scrolling through Netflix only to give up and watch something you've already seen? You're not alone. Key Away solves the endless browsing problem by letting you search for movies and shows using descriptive keywords that match your mood.

Type in words like "90s, peppy, iconic" or "cerebral sci-fi, slow-burn" and instantly discover content that fits exactly what you're looking for. No more decision fatigue. No more wasted time. Just the perfect watch, every time.

Key Features

  • Mood-Based Discovery Search using descriptive tags like "thrilling," "heartfelt," or "quirky" to find content that matches how you feel right now.
  • Quick Picks When you don't want to scroll forever, get instant recommendations based on trending searches and your viewing history.
  • Shared Lists Create watchlists with friends and vote on what to watch together. No more group chat debates.
  • Smart Learning The more you search, the better Key Away gets at understanding your unique taste and preferences.
